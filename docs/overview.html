BEFORE YOU BEGIN
The questions worth asking.
Clear expectations make a better first session—and a better presentation.
Is MCS another name for JPEG?
No. MCS is a custom educational codec. Standard JPEG is a separate interoperable export route.
Does keeping one eighth of coefficients make a file eight times smaller?
No. Coefficient count, raw RGB bytes and complete encoded size are different quantities. Headers and coding behavior matter.
What do MSE and PSNR tell me?
They measure pixel error against the working reference. They do not guarantee perceptual quality or full color-management accuracy.
An MCS file does not open in another image viewer. Why?
MCS is this lab's educational format, not standard JPEG. Decode it in the laboratory or export interoperable JPEG or PNG for other applications.
